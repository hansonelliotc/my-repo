Using the keyboard
The arrow keys change x1 and x2.
The WASD keys change b1 and b2.
Hold SHIFT while doing the above to apply velocity.
Press SPACE to reset all velocities to zero.
Press R to go to a random game.

There are four other ways to change the current game:

1. Click or drag in the main figure (left).

2. Click or drag in the global figure (top right).

3. Use the buttons in the Navigation tab.

4. Click in the large diagram (bottom right) to go to a nearby extremal game.
